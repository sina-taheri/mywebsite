Hello, welcome to my website. My name is Sina Mohammad-Taheri, I am a postdoctoral fellow at the department of Mathematics and Statistics of Concordia University, jointly jointly supervised by Drs Simone Brugiapaglia (Concordia University) and Ben Adcock (Simone Fraser University). 

Before that, I was a PhD Student at the same department under the supervision of Simone Brugiapaglia. 

My background is in biomedical signal processing and I graduated in a MSc. from department of Computer Science and biomedical engineering at University of Shiraz. I was (and still am!) fascinated by the mathematical foundations of signal processing and machine learning.

My research interests broadly include mathematical foundations of signal processing and machine learning, motivated by concrete applications, especially in health and biomedics. More particularly
  * Compressive sensing and sparse recovery.
  * Applied harmonic analysis.
  * Mathematical foundations of neural networks.

I've recently developed interests in topology and partial differential equations, and am keen to explore these topics further.
